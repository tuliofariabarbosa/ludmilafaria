How do we collect user and visitor personal data?

It's important to us that you have control over your information and know how to manage your privacy. User and visitor personal data is collected as follows:

  • When users access the contact form to request quotes, they provide us with personal information including their name, email address, and contact phone number. We also collect the content the user creates when accessing the form, in this case, a brief explanation of the situation that led to the need to use our services.
  • When a user or visitor accesses the website www.ludmilafaria.com.br, the company collects interaction and access information to ensure a better user and visitor experience. This data may include keywords used in a search, the sharing of a specific document, comments, page views, profiles, the URL from which the user or visitor originated, the browser they use, and their IP addresses, among other information that may be stored and retained.

For what purpose do we use user and visitor personal data?

The user and visitor personal data collected and stored by the website www.ludmilafaria.com.br is intended for:

  • Preparation of personalized quotes: to allow specialists to understand the problems faced by the user so that they can analyze the case and develop effective solution proposals.
  • User and visitor well-being: to improve the product and/or service offered, facilitate, streamline, and fulfill the commitments established between the user and the company, improve the user experience, and provide specific features based on the user's basic characteristics.
  • Platform improvements: to understand how the user uses the website's services to assist in business and technical development.
  • Advertising: to present personalized advertisements to the user based on the data provided.
  • Commercial: the data will be used to personalize the content offered and provide support for improving the quality and functionality of the services offered.
  • User profile prediction: automated processing of personal data to evaluate platform usage. Contract data: to provide legal certainty for the parties and facilitate the conclusion of the transaction.
  • The processing of personal data for purposes not covered by this Privacy Policy will only occur upon prior notice to the user, so that the rights and obligations set forth herein remain applicable.

How long is personal data stored?

  • Data may be removed or anonymized at the user's request, except in cases where the law provides for other processing.
  • Furthermore, according to Article 16 of the LGPD, users' personal data may be retained after the processing has ended, in the following cases:
    • I - compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation by the controller;
    • II - study by a research organization, ensuring, whenever possible, the anonymization of personal data;
    • III - transfer to a third party, provided that the data processing requirements set forth in the Law are met; or
    • IV - exclusive use by the controller, with third-party access prohibited, and provided the data is anonymized.

Cookies or Navigation Data

Cookies are text files sent by the platform to the user's or visitor's computer and stored there, containing information related to website navigation. This information is related to access data, such as location and time of access, and is stored by the user's or visitor's browser so that the platform server can read it and subsequently personalize the platform's services.
The user and visitor of the website www.ludmilafaria.com.br acknowledges and accepts that a navigation data collection system may be used through the use of cookies.
The Google AdSense service we use to serve advertising uses a DoubleClick cookie to serve more relevant ads across the web and limit the number of times a given ad is shown to you.
For more information about Google AdSense, see the official Google AdSense Privacy FAQs.
We use advertisements to offset the costs of operating this website and provide funding for future development. The behavioral advertising cookies used by this site are designed to ensure we provide you with the most relevant ads possible by anonymously tracking your interests and presenting similar things that may be of interest to you.

Several partners advertise on our behalf, and affiliate tracking cookies simply allow us to see if our customers have come to the site through one of our partner sites so that we can credit them appropriately and, where applicable, allow our affiliate partners to provide any incentive they may provide you for making a purchase.
