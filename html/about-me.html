Hi people!

I'm Ludmila Faria, a legal professional with over 7 years of experience in corporate administration, regulatory matters, and legal compliance. Born in Brazil, I began developing my negotiation skills early on through small trade experiences while selling goods. My curiosity and drive for strategy became the spark that shaped my journey in the legal field.

Connecting legal knowledge with business strategy has been my greatest strength.

At 19, I left my hometown to pursue a Bachelor’s in Law, driven by curiosity about how large corporations are structured, how they make strategic decisions, and how they address challenges. Growing up as a millennial, I witnessed firsthand the transformation of manual services into technological solutions, which sparked my passion for organizations that embrace innovation and quickly adapt to the evolving needs of society, especially by leveraging technology. After years of providing tailored compliance guidance to small and medium-sized clients - conducting due diligence, supporting legal enforcement, and aligning advice with regulatory requirements and business objectives - I stepped into Digital Law in a multi-jurisdictional environment across Brazil. Daily engagement in high-level legal discussions on technology and collaboration with cross-functional teams to refine strategies shaped my expertise and elevated my career. I later moved to Europe, where I entered the compliance field, handling contract renewal procedures, carrying out KYC onboarding checks, providing advisory support to stakeholders, and implementing documentation processes to ensure full regulatory adherence.

Business success in the digital era demands not only legal protection but also robust compliance practices

My work goes beyond mere legal consultancy, proactively adopting a vision oriented toward client success — identifying challenges and opportunities for your business in the digital world, ensuring not only legal compliance but also client satisfaction, thus preventing future legal liabilities. Navigating regulations doesn’t have to be hard — a reliable professional can guide you through legal matters.
